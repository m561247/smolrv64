## 0f633992, twice (2026-10-14)

The next main, 0f633992 (the store queue's PA in two LUTRAM copies, speculative forwarding), ran on
the same board twice, each on a fresh boot (https://browser.geekbench.com/v5/cpu/24719129 and
https://browser.geekbench.com/v5/cpu/24719450): 1,191,200,110,247 and 1,190,609,317,246 cycles,
-0.05% between them and +0.30% / +0.25% against 94b9b793; the suite's wall time 7,222.7 and
7,221.8 s. Between the two boots of one bitstream, SQLite's single-core time moved +6.2% (42.2 ->
44.9 s) and HTML5's -4.5% (34.8 -> 33.2 s), and the I$ missed 1,236 M and 1,250 M times (94b9b793's
run 1,171 M, caa5d9d3's 1,264 M): each boot's physical layout. A difference between two bitstreams
measured once each is noise below about 0.3% of the run's cycles, or about 6% of a short subtest's
time; speculative forwarding's checkpoint-slice gain (Navigation -1.02%) is below what one GB5 run
resolves.
