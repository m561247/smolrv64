# SmolRV64 -- Geekbench 5.4.1 (RISC-V preview), 2026-10-20 (second run), load-use 3 into an address

Board build of github main f644696c: an 8-byte integer load M starts in its own cycle wakes the
address generators waiting on it at its compare, and they issue on the D$'s answer before it is
known to have hit (their address stands on the D$'s registered word that it answered; else it is
not delivered). A dependent load's address is ready 3 cycles after its producer's (the gate's
`chase2`, user mode; 4 before). 166.67 MHz, WNS 0.000 ns (last-mile physopt), gate PASS on the
4 GiB board; Ubuntu over NFS, run alone under `perf stat` (the `td` set, every counter at 100%); no
faults. Multi-core 17 (one core).

single-core section's wall time: inside the run's noise. Address generators are at most 35% of the
consumers a hit's early wake would release on GB5; the rest wait for full load-hit speculation.
