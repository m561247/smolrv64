The SimPoints over-predict the FP subtests on the board: their testbench's DDR model draws read and
write latencies independently, so a write costs a concurrent read stream nothing there, where the
board's one DRAM bus charges ~10 cycles a line (a 4 MiB copy: 4.49 cycles an element on the board,
3.42 in the lockstep), and it lacks the board's 5-cycle clock crossing on every read. The image and
matrix kernels read one buffer while writing another; the integer subtests' Top-Down matches the
sim's within a few points.
