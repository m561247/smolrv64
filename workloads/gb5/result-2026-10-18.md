The headline scores hold. Eight subtests gain a point, and the rates rise 2-8% in the integer
subtests but Navigation, which does not move (its 140 G checkpoint slice took 11.9% fewer cycles:
one slice of a subtest overstates it). N-Body Physics' -1.3% is inside the board's run-to-run
spread; the rates printed to two places cannot resolve Camera, Face Detection, Speech Recognition
and Machine Learning.

## The whole run

1 h 51 min wall (6,647.6 s); 1,270,012,068,342 instructions in 1,094,626,291,350 cycles: **IPC
1.160**, -2.23% cycles against 95189924 for the same work. Per 1000 instructions: 5.80 D$ line
fills, 0.75 I$ misses, 2.39 conditional-branch and 0.21 jalr redirects (5.81, 0.76, 2.40, 0.22).
